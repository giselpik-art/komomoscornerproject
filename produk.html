Kegiatan bazar dan pentas seni SMP Santa Ursula Jakarta yang berlangsung :

Hari dan Tanggal : Selasa, 3 Februari dan Kamis, 5 Februari 2026
Waktu : 08.00 - 13.35
Tempat : SMP Santa Ursula Jakarta

Mengangkat tema “SMARANA: Semarak Budaya Indonesia Menghidupkan Cerita Rakyat”, yang berfokus pada cerita rakyat Indonesia untuk mengingat kembali dan melestarikan warisan budaya bangsa Indonesia. Sehingga melalui kegiatan tersebut, para siswi dapat menggali kembali kekayaan cerita rakyat Nusantara dan menghidupkannya kembali secara kreatif melalui pertunjukan seni, dekorasi, dan penjualan produk-produk bazar. Oleh karena itu, kelompok kami mendapatkan tema cerita rakyat Nusantara “Legenda Pulau Komodo”. Meskipun kelompok kami mengangkat cerita rakyat “Legenda Pulau Komodo”, kami tidak membuat design yang menggambarkan pulau komodo sepenuhnya seperti hutan dan pulau. Sebaliknya, kami mengembangkan tema bazar kami dengan ide, design, dan kreativitas kami yaitu nuansa awan dan suasana “dreamy”, tetapi tetap mengikuti tema “Legenda Pulau Komodo”.

Selama 4 minggu, kami berdiskusi untuk memutuskan produk yang akan kami jual sambil melihat proses dan kesulitan yang kira-kira akan dihadapi. Kami juga mempertimbangkan setiap produk sesuai dengan kriteria penentuan seperti kemenarikannya, sedang tren, sesuai dengan selera atau minat pembeli, dll. Pada akhirnya, kami memutuskan untuk menjual 11 jenis produk, termasuk makanan, minuman, dan barang. Berikut adalah produk-produk kami! 🤩⭐ :

How to Make Frozen Yogurt
(Biotech)

Equipment :

  • - Bowl
  • - Spoon
  • - Measuring cup
  • - Strainer
  • - Filter Cloth
  • - Pan
  • - Mixer

Ingredients :

  • - 1 tbsp Greek Yogurt
  • - 500ml Fresh Milk
  • - 100mlCondensed Milk
  • - Fruits (strawberries, bananas, and mangoes)
  • - Chocolate Syrup

Steps :

  • 1. First, all the tools and ingredients have been prepared beforehand.
  • 2. Second, fresh milk is poured into the pan and is heated until it starts to boil.
  • 3. Third, turn off the stove when the milk starts to rise.
  • 4. Then, pour the milk into a bowl and let it sit until lukewarm.
  • 5. Next, put the greek yogurt into the milk and mix well.
  • 6. After that, pour the mixture back into the pan and stir until combined. Then transfer it to another bowl and cover it with a cloth and put it inside a cupboard.
  • 7. Then, the yogurt is left to be fermented for 12 hours.
  • 8. After the yogurt is fermented, the yogurt is strained using a strainer to remove the excess liquid for four hours in the fridge.
  • 9. After that, mix the greek yogurt with cream and condensed milk using a mixer for 8 minutes.
  • 10. Lastly, pour the ice cream mixture into cups and put them into the freezer. Your ice cream is ready to eat!

How to Make Eco-Friendly Scented Candles
(Eco-Friendly Product)

Equipment :

  • - Ceramic seashell candle holder
  • - 2 Heat-resistant containers
  • - Stove
  • - Spoon
  • - Wick
  • - Toothpick

Ingredients :

  • - 250g Beeswax or palmwax
  • - 3-4 drops of Jasmine fragrance oil
  • - Pink dye
  • - Gold glitter

Steps :

  • 1. First, all the tools and ingredients that are needed are prepared beforehand.
  • 2. Second, the ceramic seashell holder is cleaned of dust and dried completely.
  • 3. After that, the beeswax is placed into the top of the heat resistant bowl and using the double boil technique and is heated until it is fully melted.
  • 4. Fourth, a thermometer is used to check that the wax temperature is maintained between 65°C and 75°C.
  • 5. Then, wait for the beeswax to be melted completely until it has turned into a liquid and the color is clear.
  • 6. The metal base of the wick is dipped into a small amount of melted wax and is pressed firmly onto the bottom-center of the shell.
  • 7. The pink dye is added to the melted beeswax and is stirred until the color is evenly distributed.
  • 8. Then, the jasmine essential oil is poured into the mixture and is stirred gently for one minute.
  • 9. The top of the wick is secured using a toothpick to keep it centered.
  • 10. The pink wax is poured slowly into the ceramic seashell until it is nearly full.
  • 11. After that, the wax is monitored until a thin matte film starts to form on the surface
  • 12. Gold glitter is sprinkled over the soft wax before it hardens completely.
  • 13. The candle is left to sit at room temperature for a few hours until it is completely solidified.
  • 14. Lastly, the wick is trimmed to a length of 1 cm and the candle is ready to be used.
